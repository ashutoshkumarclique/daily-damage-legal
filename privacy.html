Daily Damage Privacy Policy

Last updated: Pending final publication date

Daily Damage is designed to keep expense journaling local, private, and user controlled.

What the app does

Daily Damage lets you manually record expenses, manage a monthly budget, and view weekly or monthly roast-style summaries based on the entries you create.

Data storage

  • Expense entries are stored locally on your device.
  • Budget data is stored locally on your device.
  • App preferences such as currency and selected character are stored locally on your device.
  • The app does not require account creation or login.
  • The app does not provide cloud sync in the current release.

Google Play Billing

Daily Damage offers a lifetime Premium unlock through Google Play Billing. Purchases are processed by Google Play. The app receives billing status, product details, and purchase entitlement information needed to unlock Premium characters, but it does not receive your full payment card details.

What the app does not do

  • No login
  • No backend developer server
  • No SMS reading
  • No OCR or receipt scanning
  • No bank linking
  • No cloud sync
  • No automatic mood notifications
  • No ads in the current build
  • No third-party analytics SDK in the current build
  • CSV export only when you choose to export
  • No automatic XLSX or PDF export in the current release
  • No sale of financial data

Sharing

The app can generate share card images from your reports and CSV files from your expense entries.

  • Images are created only when you choose to save or share them.
  • CSV files are created only when you choose to export them.
  • Saving to gallery, exporting CSV, and sharing are user initiated.
  • The app does not automatically publish or transmit your content.

Permissions

The app uses only the permissions needed for its local functionality, Google Play Billing, and user-initiated image saving or sharing on supported Android versions.

Financial guidance disclaimer

Daily Damage is not a financial advisory service. Roast content and report content are provided for entertainment and self-awareness only.
